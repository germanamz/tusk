## What

Build `internal/lint/blankline/` with `analyzer.go`,
`analyzer_test.go`, and `testdata/src/a/a.go`. The analyzer detects
missing blank lines around `if err != nil` (and `<noun>Err != nil`)
guards: between an error-producing assignment and its guard, and
between the guard's closing brace and the next statement. Test
files use `test *testing.T` from the start (rule 4 applies to
analyzer test code).

## Why

Rule 2 enforcement. Dense unspaced blocks make short identifiers
visually easy to lose; even with rule 1 in place, spacing matters.
